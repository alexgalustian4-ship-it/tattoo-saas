Questions, answered

Do unused credits roll over?

Yes — on paid plans, any credits you don't use stay in your balance and stack with next month's credits. Nothing is ever taken away from you.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Manage or cancel your subscription in one click from your account — no emails, no phone calls. You keep the credits you already have.

Is the payment secure?

Payments are processed by Stripe, the same infrastructure used by Amazon and Shopify. We never see or store your card details.

Do I own the designs I create?

Yes — every design you generate is yours. Download it in high resolution, bring it to your tattoo artist, print the stencil. It's your art.

What's the difference between plans and credit packs?

Plans renew automatically each month with fresh credits at the best rate. Credit packs are a one-time purchase — perfect for topping up whenever you need a few more generations.
