Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Ink Studio AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service at inkstudio.ai.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Images you upload — body photos and reference images you provide for tattoo generation or rendering. These are processed and then deleted from our servers within 24 hours.
  • Prompts and inputs — text descriptions, style choices, and quiz answers you enter to generate designs.
  • Usage data — anonymized logs of features used, generation counts, and errors, for service improvement.
  • Account data (if you create an account) — email address and hashed password.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To generate tattoo designs and skin renderings as requested
  • To improve our AI models and generation quality (using anonymized aggregate data only)
  • To send account-related emails (password resets, billing receipts)
  • To detect and prevent abuse

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your uploaded photos for advertising or profiling.

3. Third-Party AI Processing

Your prompts and images are sent to Higgsfield AI for processing. Higgsfield acts as a data processor under our instructions. Images are used solely to fulfill your generation request and are not retained by Higgsfield beyond the processing window.

4. Data Storage

Generated designs are stored temporarily and accessible via your session. If you do not have an account, designs are stored in your browser's local storage only. Uploaded images are deleted from our servers within 24 hours of your session ending.

5. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for the service to function (session management, authentication). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@inkhay.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify registered users by email of material changes. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at contact@inkhay.com.
